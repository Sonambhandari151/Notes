Small business and home routers typically have two primary types of ports
1. **Ethernet Ports:** These ports connect to the Internal Switch portion of the routers. These ports are usually labelled as "ETHERNET" or "LAN" . All devices connected to the switch ports are on the same local network.
     1. - Connects wired devices (PCs, gaming consoles, etc.) to the router.
    - Used for internal network communication.
    - Usually multiple ports, labeled **LAN 1, LAN 2, ...**.
2. **Internet Port**: This port is used to connect the device to another network. Often used to connect to the cable or DSL modem in order to access the internet.
    - Connects your router to the modem or directly to your ISP’s network.
- Used for internet access.
- Usually a single port, labeled **WAN** or **Internet**.

In addition to the wired ports, many home routers include a **radio antenna** and a built-in **wireless access point.** By default, the wireless devices are on the same local network as the devices that are physically plugged into the LAN switch Ports. The internet port is the only port that is on a different network in the default configuration.
